## INTERACTION SCENE — constrained outcomes — 1:50

*The Dev confrontation runs on the interaction-agent schema (branch-map §9).
Outcomes: **A** — full confession (canonical, below) · **B** — denial-then-
break: Dev holds out until Riya plays Sana's last voicemail, then collapses
into the same confession · **FALLBACK** — sustained deflection: Riya plays
the recorder-theft card ("woh seedha almari tak gaya, Dev — yeh baat sirf
teen log jaante the: main, tu, aur ek murda ladki") → break → confession.
All outcomes converge on `dev=turned` + the godown plan. No path exits this
scene without the confession.*

[SFX: college media lab, empty campus — holiday. A door. Kachori paper-bag
rustle. The Khoji recorder's switch: CLICK. Its little red light hum.]

DEV: (cheerful, entering) Diwali se pehle interview? Tu bhi na... maine
kachori—

RIYA: Baith.

DEV: (beat) ...Yeh record ho raha hai?

RIYA: Khoji ka naya episode. Title soch rahi hoon. "Ghar Ka Bhedi" kaisa
rahega?

[SFX: the paper bag, set down very slowly]

DEV: (careful laugh) Riya, kal se tu ajeeb messages kar rahi hai. Pehle
Mausi ke ghar, ab yahan— kya chal raha hai?

RIYA: Parson raat mere ghar mein ek aadmi tha. Power cut karke aaya tha.
Kuch nahi le gaya. Sirf ek cheez dhoondh raha tha — yeh. (she taps the
recorder) Bina bhatke. Seedha almari tak.

DEV: Toh... chor hoga, showroom mein bhi pichhle mahine—

RIYA: Maine tujhe kab bataya tha ki main Sana ka case phir se khol rahi
hoon?

DEV: (pause too long) Tune... tune nahi bataya.

RIYA: Sahi jawab. Maine bataya tha. Ek hi insaan ko. Chai ke saath,
tumhari bike pe, September mein. Maine kaha tha — "Dev, main lagbhag
pahunch gayi hoon. Hearing se pehle bhej dungi sab kuch."

[SFX: silence. The recorder hum grows enormous.]

DEV: Riya—

RIYA: (not loud — worse) Ek cheez sunayegi tujhe. (phone tap)
SANA (recording, tinny, bright, ghost-young): "—Riyu yaar, kal subah
chalna na saath, akeli jaungi toh Ammi ko tension—" (cut)
RIYA: Uski aakhri voicemail. Gyarah mahine se roz sunti hoon. (beat) Aur
parson raat, jis aadmi ne mera darwaza khatkhataya, woh unhi logon ka tha
jinhone Sana ko sadak pe chhod diya tha. Toh main poochhungi, Dev. Ek
baar. Sirf ek baar. (beat) Tune kisko bataya?

DEV: (breaking, fast) Maine socha main teri madad kar raha hoon — Riya
meri baat sun — Tyagi ji ne... unhone kaha file band karwa denge, koi
case-vase nahi, bas... bas thoda—

RIYA: Bol.

DEV: (the sentence he'll hear forever) Unhone kaha sirf daraayenge, Riya.
Sirf daraayenge.

[SFX: her chair scrapes back. Footsteps to the window. A long exhale.]

DEV: (wrecked, small) Papa ki dealership Chaddha ke ek dastakht pe chalti
hai. Pichhle saal renewal roka tha toh Papa ne khaana chhod diya tha. Main
sirf... main chahta tha tu ruk jaye. Zinda rahe. Mujhe nahi pata tha ki
woh log... (he can't finish)

RIYA: (quiet, to the window) Tujhe pata hai maine tujhe sab kuch kyun
nahi bataya tha, Dev? Taaki tu bacha rahe. (turns) Aur tune wahi cheez
unko de di jo main tujhse bacha rahi thi. Mera naam.

DEV: (weeping) Riya, main—

RIYA: Rona band. (beat) Tu ab mera aadmi hai, samjha?

DEV: (lost) ...Kya?

RIYA: Maafi baad mein maangna — kamaa ke. Aaj raat Kalia godown pe piyega.
Diwali-eve pe har saal peeta hai. Tu bhi wahin hoga. English le jaana —
Papa ki dukaan se. Aur apna phone... jeb mein. Chalu.

[SFX: the recorder CLICKS off.]

RIYA: Episode abhi record hona baaki hai.

[SFX: a small plastic click — the SD card out of the recorder; a zip;
her jacket's inner pocket]

RIYA (quiet, to herself): Aur asli copy ab yahan rahegi. Jahan main,
wahan yeh.

## SCENE 3 — THE GODOWN STING — 6:00

[SFX: night. Her room. Two phones on the desk — one is a live call, its
speaker tinny: the godown comes to us THROUGH Dev's pocket. Bottles.
A shutter half-down. Men laughing. Diwali-eve crackers, sporadic.]

⚡[volume_duck t≈6:05 to 0.5 — the room ducks; the tinny pocket-feed is the
whole world now]

NARRATOR: Raat ko Riya ne apna phone Arjun ki line se joda, aur Dev ka
phone... apni jeb se godown pahuncha diya. Ab teen log ek jeb se sun rahe
the.

KALIA: (drunk, expansive) Chhote! ENGLISH! Aaj toh tu aadmi ban gaya!

DEV: (steadier than he has any right to be) Diwali hai, Kalia bhai.

[SFX: glasses, a plastic chair groans under a big man]

KALIA: Ye showroom, chhote, sab dikhawa hai. Asli kaam? Asli kaam Kalia
karta hai. Bade log gaadi chalate hain... (laughs) ...sambhalta Kalia hai.

DEV: (bait, light) Sambhalna matlab? Aap toh driver—

KALIA: Driver?! (slams glass) Pichhle saal ek headlight tooti thi.
Chhabbis hazaar ka bill — woh sab ko dikhta hai. Watchman ka muh — pachaas
hazaar. CCTV wali disk — muft. Qureshi file — teen mahine mein "untraced."
(beat, proud) Untraced. Bol, driver ka kaam hai ye?

[SFX: in her room — Riya's breath stops. Arjun on the line, silent.]

DEV: (carefully) Aur ab? Ab toh sab shaant hai na, bhai?

KALIA: (pouring, easy) Kal raat ke baad. Ek scooty wali ladki ka kaam bhi
ho jayega, phir aaraam. (drinks) Diwali ki raat ka fayda pata hai kya
hai, chhote? Pataakhon mein... kya scooty, kya cheekh. Sab ek jaisa
sunai deta hai.

[SFX: a long, drunk laugh. Nobody joins it. Far off, a rocket whistles up
and cracks.]

rakhna, chhote. Bahar... bahut dhuaan hai aajkal.

## SCENE 4 — PRESENT SIDE: THE DATE — 8:20

[SFX: present. Cyber-cell office, night. A ceiling fan. Files slap on a
desk, one after another. Chai slurp.]

ZOYA: Sana Qureshi. FIR — teen mahine mein "untraced vehicle." Watchman

ZOYA: (pause — the slurp stops) Phir mera haath ruk gaya. Rohan Chaddha.
Anticipatory bail. Application teen hafte pehle file hui thi. Hearing ki

minute mein. Pata hai kyun gyaarah minute, Arjun? Kyunki SP office mein
ek anonymous complaint pending thi — audio evidence ki list ke saath. Aur
us complaint ka complainant... hearing se aath ghante pehle flyover se
gir gaya.

[SFX: the fan. Just the fan.]

ARJUN: (barely) Riya complaint kar chuki thi.

ZOYA: Teen hafte pehle. Isliye application teen hafte pehle. Dekho
Arjun — maine bhoot-pret pe kabhi yakeen nahi kiya, aur is file mein bhi
koi bhoot nahi hai. Yeh koi bhatakti hui maut nahi hai, Arjun. Iske paas
TAREEKH hai. (beat) Aur tareekh talti nahi. Jab tak complaint zinda hai,
complainant khatre mein hai. Tumhari behen kismat se nahi haari thi.
Deadline se haari thi.

NARRATOR: Teen din se Arjun jise "honi" samajh raha tha... uska naam
court ke kaagaz pe likha tha. Hearing: Diwali ke agle din. Riya ke paas
poori raat thi. Aur Rohan Chaddha ke paas... sirf wahi ek raat.
