## COLD OPEN — 0:00

[SFX: evening bazaar — Chhoti Diwali. Anaar fizzing somewhere. Traffic. Then,
glass-muffled: showroom PA testing — "hello, mic check, ek do teen—"]

NARRATOR: Diwali se ek din pehle. Chhoti Diwali. MP Nagar mein ek imaarat
aisi jagmagati thi jaise usne kabhi koi galat kaam dekha hi na ho. Chaddha
Motors. Kaanch ki chaar manzilein. Neeche showroom — jhaalar, diye, staff
ki Diwali party ki taiyyari. Aur upar, mezzanine pe, ek kamra jispe likha
tha: **Records — Accounts.**

NARRATOR: Kaanch ke mahal mein sab kuch dikhta hai. Bas sach nahi.

[SFX: cut to Arjun's room. Ceiling fan. Papers. A chai slurp — Zoya's.]

NARRATOR: Teesri raat tak window ne rules maanne band kar diye the. Line ab
bas... khuli rehti thi. Ek kamre mein do waqt saans le rahe the — mez pe
Zoya ki files, aaj ki. Phone mein Riya ki scooty, teen din purani.

ZOYA (flat, tapping paper): Dekho Arjun — nagar nigam ke fire-safety
records se nikala hai. Chaddha Motors ka NOC plan. Building nahi badalti,
Arjun. Seedhi B — kitchen ke peeche — mezzanine. Records office yahan.

⚡[message_overlay t≈1:10 — image lands from **SI Zoya Khan**: a stamped
floor plan, "CHADDHA MOTORS — FIRE EVACUATION LAYOUT", one corridor
highlighted]

ARJUN (on the open line): Riyu. Naksha aa gaya.

RIYA (on scooty, wind on mic, grinning audibly): Aur mere paas entry hai.
College fest ka lanyard, kaali kurti, aur ek crate diye. "Sharma Events."
Bhaiya — aaj raat unki party mein ek waitress extra hogi.

[SFX: scooty slows. Kills. A quieter street — college gate chowkidar radio.]

RIYA (softer): Ek minute. Pehle... ek cheez leni hai.

## SCENE 2 — THE MEDIA LAB — 1:50

[SFX: corridor echo. A door unlocked with a borrowed key. Juice cartons
shifted. A tin box opens.]

NARRATOR: Khoji ka recorder wahi tha jahan hamesha rehta tha — media lab
ki almari mein, juice ke khaali dabbon ke peeche. Riya ne SD card nikala.
Ek saal ki raatein. Ek saal ka sach. Watchman ki awaaz. Garage manager ki
hansi. Sana ki aakhri voicemail.

RIYA (quiet, to the card): Sana ke liye shuru kiya tha. Aaj... apne liye
poora karungi.

[SFX: the card zips into an inner jacket pocket. Her phone buzzes — once,
twice.]

⚡[message_overlay t≈2:25 — **Dev ❤️**: "riya kahan ho? uncle aaye the
showroom pe. call karo na please" — her thumb hovers — swipes it away]

NARRATOR: Teesra din tha Dev ke messages ka. Aur teesra din tha... unka
jawab na jaane ka. Kuch sawaal aise hote hain jinka jawab maangne se
darr lagta hai — kyunki jawab ke baad kuch bhi pehle jaisa nahi rehta.

ARJUN (careful): Tu theek hai?

RIYA (too quickly): Haan. Chal. Party shuru hone se pehle ghusna hai.

✦[if watcher=knows_moves — insert: NARRATOR: Is baar Riya ne kisi ko nahi
bataya tha ki woh kahan ja rahi hai. Maa ko bhi nahi. Jab ghar ka bhedi
ho... toh ghar se bhi chupna padta hai.]

## SCENE 3 — THE BREAK-IN — 3:00

[SFX: showroom service gate. Crates, shouting, dhol loading. A harried
supervisor.]

GUARD: Aye — ruk. Kaun?

RIYA (bright, bored, chewing gum): Sharma Events. Diye kahan rakhne hain?

GUARD: ...Andar. Catering waalon se milo, stage ke peeche. Jaldi.

[SFX: she's in. Party noise swells, then muffles as she slips past the
kitchen — steam, frying, a radio. A stairwell door clicks shut behind her.
Sudden quiet. Her breath, close on the mic now.]

⚡[volume_duck t≈3:40 to 0.35, ramp 600ms — the whole mix drops to her whisper]

RIYA (whisper): Seedhi B. Chadh rahi hoon.

ARJUN (low, eyes on the plan): Upar pahunch ke — teen kadam aage. Right pe
fire extinguisher. Uske baad waali door. Wahi hai.

✦[if watcher=seen_bike — insert, at the stairwell window:
RIYA (whisper, stopping): Bhaiya. Neeche parking mein. Kaali bike. *Wahi*
aadhi number plate. Yahin khadi hai — ghar ke saamne wali raat waali.
ARJUN: Matlab woh yahin kaam karta hai. Photo le. Chal ab. Rukna mat.]

✦[if trust=shaky — insert, on the mezzanine landing:
ARJUN: Left, Riyu.
RIYA (whisper): Pakka? Pichhli baar bhi tumne bola tha aur—
[SFX: she pushes the RIGHT door a hand's width — party floor light and
laughter slice in, a waiter's tray passing an arm's length away —]
RIYA (breath held; door eased back): ...Theek hai. Theek hai. Ab se sirf
tumhari sunungi.]

[SFX: the records office door. Handle turns — unlocked. Inside: AC hum,
paper smell you can hear — stillness.]

NARRATOR: Party ke din accounts waale cash box leke neeche the. Kamra
khula tha. Kismat nahi thi yeh — Arjun ki teen din purani hindsight thi:
Chaddha Motors ki apni Diwali reel, jo uske waqt mein teen din pehle post
ho chuki thi. Har reel ek CCTV hai... jo pehle se internet pe hai.

ARJUN: Unki apni reel ke hisaab se — 8:41 pe MLA ka kaafila gate pe
aayega. Sab bahar honge. Tere paas pandrah minute hain.

[SFX: register pages flipping — heavy ledger, fast careful turns]

RIYA (whisper, reading): "Vehicle Repair Register"... Fortuner... Fortuner...
[page. page. Then — her breath stops]
Bhaiya. **Fortuner. Front bumper. Headlight assembly. Denting, painting.**
Date... [she exhales, shaking] ...Sana ke *agle din* ki. Agle hi din.

NARRATOR: Ek saal se jis panne ko dhoondh rahi thi, woh ek register mein
chupchaap pada tha — jaise sach ko bhi Diwali ki chhutti mil gayi ho.

⚡[message_overlay t≈5:20 — her camera shutter UI: three photos snap —
ledger page, register cover, the room's file rack]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS — leather soles on mezzanine wood, approaching]

⚡[haptic t≈5:35 — heartbeat_rising, 8s]

RIYA (barely air): Koi aa raha hai—

[SFX: the clerk enters humming, rummages — a stapler lifted off the desk a
metre from her held breath behind the rack — hums out. Door. Silence.]

RIYA (a tiny broken laugh of relief): ...Stapler. Woh stapler lene aaya tha.

## SCENE 4 — THE SPIKE: ROHAN — 6:00

[SFX: below — PA feedback. A flurry. Staff voices: "Sir aa gaye — Rohan
sir aa gaye — jaldi jaldi—"]

NARRATOR: Woh jaldi aa gaya tha.

[SFX: and for the first time in this story — HIS voice. Warm. Big. The
voice of a man everyone laughs with one second before they're told to.]

⚡[volume_duck t≈6:10 to 0.3 — the party swallows itself; his voice sits alone]
⚡[screen_dim t≈6:12 to 50% — hold while he speaks]

ROHAN (below, glad-handing): Arre arre arre — kaam roko mat yaar, main
koi mehmaan thodi hoon! (laughter) Bunty! AC full. Mehmaanon ko Bhopal ki
garmi nahi — Chaddha ki thandak yaad rehni chahiye. (laughter swells)

NARRATOR: Riya louvre waale darwaze ke peechhe thi. Do manzil ka kaanch
neeche. Aur seedhiyon ke paas, halki si awaaz mein, wohi aadmi—

ROHAN (low, flat — the warmth gone like a switch): Kalia. Woh chhoti
waali baat? ...Nahi. Kal raat tak sab saaf. Hearing ke baad koi kahani
nahi bachni chahiye. Kisi ki bhi.

[beat]

ROHAN (bright again, clapping once): Chalo! Diya jalao bhai!

[SFX: cheering below. Her breath, held so long it shakes on release.]

NARRATOR: Riya ne suna. Arjun ne samjha. "Chhoti waali baat" unnees saal
ki thi... aur us waqt darwaze ke doosri taraf saans rok ke baithi thi.

## SCENE 5 — THE EXIT — 7:15

[SFX: stairwell, quick soft steps; kitchen steam; the service corridor]

NARRATOR: Neeche utri. Kitchen. Corridor. Bees kadam aur bahar. Unnees.
Athra. ...Corridor ke mod pe, chhat ke kone mein — ek kaali aankh.

⚡[haptic t≈7:35 — knock ×1, single hard tick]

NARRATOR: Camera. Riya ka chehra, roshni mein. Ek second. Bas ek second.

[SFX: gate. Street. Chhoti Diwali crowd noise crashes in — she's out.]

RIYA (walking fast, laughing shakily into the earphone): Nikal gayi.
Bhaiya — NIKAL GAYI main! SD card, ledger, sab — [she exhales] Sana,
sun rahi hai? *Mil gaya.*

## SCENE 6 — PRESENT SIDE, LATER THAT NIGHT — 8:20

[SFX: Arjun's room. Night. Zoya's phone on speaker, a tired junior's voice.]

JUNIOR (on phone): Madam... MP Nagar thane mein aaj shaam ek complaint
lagi hai. Chaddha Motors. "Attempted theft — unidentified female —
CCTV footage preserved." Par madam, ajeeb baat—

ZOYA: Bolo.

JUNIOR: Entry aaj hui hai... complaint pe date *teen din purani* hai.

[SFX: call ends. Silence. Zoya looks up — you can hear it in her pause.]

ZOYA (quiet): Arjun. Yeh complaint kal shaam tak system mein nahi thi.
Records office se ek ghante ke andar unhone apna CCTV khud nikala tha.

NARRATOR: Ab dono jaante the. Woh jaanti thi usne kya kiya. Aur woh
jaanta tha... ki woh jaanti hai.

## SCENE 7 — THE TERRACE — 9:00

[SFX: her terrace. Night wind. Far dhol. A row of diyas guttering.]

NARRATOR: Riya terrace pe baithi thi — wahi jagah jahan se teen raat
pehle usne hanste hue phone uthaya tha. God mein recorder. Haath mein
phone. Photos mein... sab kuch.

RIYA (quiet): Sana ko bas yahi chahiye tha, bhaiya. Ek register ka panna.
Ek saal pehle mil jaata toh...

NARRATOR: Jo Sana ke liye ek saal late tha... shayad Riya ke liye ek din
pehle tha. *Shayad.*

RIYA (softer; a smile you can hear): Yaad hai tumne mujhe scooty sikhaayi
thi? Peechhe bhaagte the, aur bola tha — "haath chhod bhi doon, toh bhi
pakda hua hoon." ...Waisa hi karna kal. Haath mat chhodna.

[SFX: silence on Arjun's side — two seconds too long]

NARRATOR: Arjun ne yaad karna chaha. Scooty. Uske chhote haath handle pe.
Apni awaaz, uske peechhe bhaagti hui. ...Kuch nahi tha. Jahan woh shaam
honi chahiye thi, wahan ab sirf safedi thi. Keemat... kat chuki thi.
Aaj ke badlaav ki. Chupchaap.

ARJUN (steady, because it has to be): Nahi chhodunga.

⚡[message_overlay t≈10:00 — **Dev ❤️**: "maine kuch galat kiya hai kya?
bata toh do" — then: **Dev ❤️ calling...** — **Declined**]

NARRATOR: Neeche gali mein ek scooty guzri. Riya ne message dekha. Phone
ulta rakh diya. Kuch sawaal... kal ke baad.
