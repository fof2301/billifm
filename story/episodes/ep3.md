# EP 3 — "Parchhaai"

**Night 2, late · Riya's clock: D-2 midnight · Runtime target: ~11:00 · Ends on CP3 (THE FORK)**
Opens on the CP2 variant (CHAARA or OJHAL — first ~2 min differ; converge at
11:57 PM). Mid-episode spike: the storeroom door (≈8:10). End hook: "aakhri
poora din" + the fork (≈10:55). This is the immersion centerpiece of the
series: blackout, torch, knocks, the silence test — the listener's device is
inside the scene for four straight minutes.

Notation: `NARRATOR` is novel-style, minimal. `[SFX]` sound design.
`⚡[effect]` device effects for the event track (timestamps indicative).
`[if flag=…]` blocks are flag-conditional lines. Dialogue is the recording
script — Hinglish, spoken register.

## VARIANT — CHAARA (if CP2 = CHAARA) — 0:35

*(Riya as bait: the evening market op. Flag set: `watcher=seen_bike`.)*

[SFX: Diwali market — dhol practice, bargaining, a temple loudspeaker,
cycle bells. Riya walking, one earphone in; her breath close on the mic.]

RIYA (murmuring, lips barely moving): Bol bhaiya. Sun rahi hoon.

ARJUN (V.O., tight): Seedha chal. Rukna mat, mudna mat. Tu bas... dikhti
reh. Jo bhi hai, usse lagna chahiye tu akeli hai aur tujhe kuch nahi pata.

RIYA: Akeli toh hoon. Aur pata toh sach mein kuch nahi. (beat) Mazaak tha.
Daant mat do.

NARRATOR: Naya bazaar, Diwali se do din pehle — aadhi raat ko bhi bheed
hi bheed. Aur bheed se behtar jaal koi nahi hota. Plan seedha tha: Riya dikhegi. Peecha karne
wala peechhe aayega. Aur sabzi wali gali — jiska sirf ek hi mooh hai —
usse ugal degi.

[SFX: her footsteps turn; market din narrows into a lane — closer walls,
a hand-pump drip, gunny sacks dragged somewhere]

RIYA (whisper): Gali mein hoon. Ab?

ARJUN (V.O.): Gin. Dus kadam. Phir paan wale ke peeche ruk ke palat.

RIYA: ...Saat. Aath. Nau. (she stops; breath held; then — a slow exhale)
Bhaiya. Ek bike. Gali ke mooh pe. Wahi bike jo college ke bahar thi —
maine dhyan nahi diya tha tab. Aadmi phone dekhne ka natak kar raha hai.

ARJUN (V.O.): Photo. Zoom se. Chehra mat — **bike**. Number plate.

[SFX: camera shutter — once, twice. A sack shifts. A man's cough, close.]

RIYA (whisper, adrenaline turning into that grin you can hear): Mili.
Aadhi plate — MP-zero-four... aage bori hai. Par mili, bhaiya. Ab yeh
sirf mujhe dekhne wala nahi raha. **Ab maine bhi isse dekh liya.**

NARRATOR: Dar ka rukh badal gaya tha. Shikaar ne shikari ki tasveer
kheench li thi. ...Par shikari ko farak nahi pada. Kyunki usse sirf ek
cheez chahiye thi. Aur woh jaanta tha — woh cheez kahan dhoondhni hai.

[SFX: transition — night deepens; the market fades into a sleeping street]

NARRATOR: Raat gyarah sattavan. Ghar. Maa apne kamre mein so chuki thi.
Riya jaagti thi — aur line... line ab bhi khuli thi.

*(→ continue at SCENE: 11:57 PM)*

## VARIANT — OJHAL (if CP2 = OJHAL) — 0:35

*(Riya vanishes to Mausi's. Flag set: `watcher=knows_moves`.)*

[SFX: night — a gate unlatched carefully, held so it doesn't sing. Back
lane. An auto flagged down with a whisper, not a wave.]

NARRATOR: Gyarah baj ke bees minute. Jab mohalla blackout mein soya tha,
ek auto peechhe wali gali se Ashoka Garden ki taraf muda. Riya ne teen
baar peechhe dekha — koi nahi. Kisi ko nahi bataya tha. Maa ke sirhane
bas ek parchi — "do din Mausi ke yahan hoon, project hai."

RIYA (V.O., voice note tone, hushed): Bhaiya, pahunch gayi. Mausi ke
yahan. Kisi ko nahi pata. Mausi so chuki thi — chaabi wahi purani jagah.
(beat) Dev ko bas message kar diya — woh pareshan ho jaata hai na...
usse bhi bas itna ki Mausi ke yahan hoon. Address nahi.

NARRATOR: Ek ghar jo kisi naqshe pe nahi tha. Riya ne paani piya, saans
li — pehli poori saans us raat ki. Bees minute. Bas bees minute mili.

[SFX: crickets. A wall clock, slower than her pulse. Then — a gate
hinge, slow.]

RIYA (whisper, suddenly): Bhaiya.

ARJUN (V.O.): Kya hua?

RIYA: Gate pe... koi hai. (breath) Ruka hua hai. Upar dekh raha hai.
**Meri** khidki ki taraf. Is kamre ki. (beat — the thought lands like ice)
Bhaiya... main is ghar mein hoon, yeh **kisi ko nahi pata tha.** Maa ko
address maine likha nahi, Mausi ka number kisi ke paas nahi...

ARJUN (V.O., very quiet): ...Kisi ne bataya hai.

RIYA: Kaun batayega? Jaanta hi kaun hai mujhe itna? (silence — neither of
them says the name that just walked into both their heads)

NARRATOR: Aur wahi sawaal, jiska jawaab koi nahi dena chahta tha, gate
pe khada raha. Gyarah sattavan baje... woh saaya gate se hata. Ghar ki
taraf.

*(→ continue at SCENE: 11:57 PM. Mausi so chuki thi — neend ki goli, roz
ki aadat. Ghar mein jaagti sirf Riya thi. Aur line khuli thi.)*

## SCENE — 11:57 PM (converged; "the house") — 2:35

[SFX: a sleeping house. A wall clock. Riya's pen scratching — she's making
notes by lamplight. Her voice low, tired-wired.]

RIYA: ...toh kal subah recorder lab se nikaalti hoon, copies banati hoon,
aur phir tumhare plan ke hisaab se— (yawn) —bhaiya, yeh detective log
sote kab hain?

ARJUN (V.O., dry): Retire hone ke baad.

RIYA (small laugh): Retire toh tum abhi ho. Shaadi ki video banate ho.

ARJUN (V.O.): Haan. Aur teri shaadi ki bhi banaunga. Free mein. Bas tu—

[SFX: **CLACK.** Every appliance-hum in the house dies at once. The lamp.
The fridge. The fan winds down. True silence.]

⚡[screen_blackout t≈3:10 — listener's screen cuts to black, duration
follows the scene]

RIYA (breath): ...Light gayi.

ARJUN (V.O.): Poore mohalle ki?

RIYA (moving to the window; whisper): Nahi. Saamne wale ghar ka bulb...
jal raha hai. (beat) Sirf hamari gayi hai, bhaiya.

NARRATOR: Diwali se do din pehle, sheher bhar ke transformer haanfte hain.
Light jaana koi baat nahi thi. Par sirf **ek** ghar ki jaana... baat thi.

[SFX: downstairs — metal on metal, patient: a window latch being worked.
Then wood easing open, a chappal-less foot on floor. And a sound that will
own the next five minutes: a man's slow exhale-whistle through his teeth.
Lazy. Unbothered. Tune-less.]

RIYA (barely voiced): Bhaiya. **Ghar mein koi hai.**

## SCENE — THE HIDE — 3:50

ARJUN (V.O., instantly, cold-calm): Riya. Store room. Abhi. Daayein
corridor, seedhiyon ke neeche. Chal — **abhi.**

[if trust=shaky]
RIYA (frozen half-second): Store room?? Wahan se toh nikalne ka koi
raasta— bhaiya woh toh band jagah—
ARJUN (V.O.): **Riya.** Ek baar. Sirf ek baar meri baat maan le.
NARRATOR: Teen second. Usne teen second sochne mein liye. Aur neeche,
corridor ke mooh pe, ek torch ki roshni deewar ghoom gayi — teen second
aur ruki hoti... toh roshni uspe ghoomti.
[end if]

[SFX: her bare feet, fast and feather-light on stairs. Cloth brushing a
wall. Her breath — held, released in slivers.]

⚡[flashlight t≈4:15 — flicker ×3, then ON, low — her phone-torch cupped
in her palm is the listener's torch: the only light in the world]

NARRATOR: Uske phone ki torch — hatheli se dhaki hui, ungliyon ke beech
se utni hi roshni jitni zaroori thi. Us roshni mein duniya sirf teen
cheezein thi: seedhiyan. Darwaza. Andhera.

[SFX: a door eased open — the storeroom breathes out old jute and
mustard-oil tins. She slips in. The door draws to, not quite clicking.]

RIYA (sub-whisper): Andar hoon.

ARJUN (V.O., whisper): Torch band. Phone silent. **Abhi.**

⚡[flashlight t≈4:55 — OFF. Total dark again]
⚡[volume_duck t≈4:58 to 0.3, ramp 600ms — the world drops to whisper-level;

[SFX: her switch flicks to silent. Two heartbeats. Then — the line itself
flutters: that thin Ep1 static, rising—]

RIYA (sub-whisper, panicked): Bhaiya? Bhaiya awaaz—

[SFX: **snap.** Dead air. The link — held open all evening — buckles.]

[SFX: the storeroom door creaks open. Her feet in the wrecked house —

Closet wala studio. Bhaiya... mera closet wala studio kaun jaanta hai?

[end if]

[if watcher=knows_moves]
RIYA (walking, counting damage, voice flat with shock): Laptop le gaya.
