# EP 1 — "Missed Call"

**Night 1 · Riya's clock: D-3 · Runtime target: ~10:30 · Ends on CP1**
Mid-episode spike: the news alert during the call (≈5:00). End hook: the
scratched 'A' + "teen din" (≈10:00).

Notation: `NARRATOR` is novel-style, minimal. `[SFX]` sound design.
`⚡[effect]` device effects for the event track (timestamps indicative).
Dialogue is the recording script — Hinglish, spoken register.

## COLD OPEN — 0:00

[SFX: far-off Diwali crackers, sparse, dying out. A ceiling fan. A phone
vibrates once against wood.]

⚡[message_overlay t≈0:20 — lockscreen popup renders on listener's device:
**Riya 💜 — Missed Call (3) — 11:41 PM · 11:47 PM · 11:53 PM**]

NARRATOR: Teen missed calls. Gyarah ikatalis. Gyarah saintalis. Gyarah
trepan. Arjun Mishra ne apni zindagi mein hazaar calls uthayi thi — client
ki, bank wale ki, wrong number ki. Jo teen calls usne nahi uthayi... woh
uski behen ki aakhri teen calls thi.

[SFX: a chair creaks. A man exhales — the kind of breath that's been held
for three days.]

ARJUN (V.O., flat, to himself): Phone silent pe tha. Shaadi ki editing thi.
Deadline thi. ...Deadline.

[beat — one dry, broken almost-laugh]

NARRATOR: Diwali ke teen din baad. Mishra ghar mein ab bhi rishtedaaron ke
jhoothe glass pade the. Maa neend ki goli se soyi thi. Aur Arjun — Arjun
teesri raat se apni behen ka Instagram dekh raha tha.

## SCENE 2 — HIS ROOM, 1:50 AM — 0:55

[SFX: room tone. Phone speaker: a reel plays, tinny — RIYA's voice, bright:
"—toh doston, Khoji ke agle episode mein milte hain, tab tak ke liye — aankhen
khuli rakho!" — it loops. And loops.]

ARJUN (quiet): Aankhen khuli rakho. Hm.

NARRATOR: Uski behen ke aakhri shabd, ek reel mein. Unnees saal ki Riya
Mishra. College mein journalism. Closet mein podcast. Scooty pe... [the
narrator stops itself] Report mein likha tha — "accident."

[SFX: drawer opens. Something slides — glass on wood. A cracked phone,
placed on the desk.]

NARRATOR: Uska phone. Flyover ke neeche mila tha. Screen pe daraar aisi —
jaise bijli gir ke ruk gayi ho.

⚡[message_overlay t≈1:55 — chat thread opens on listener's device; typing
indicator: Arjun types into "Riya 💜": **"kaash maine utha liya hota"** —
the words hang — then delete, letter by letter, until the box is empty]

ARJUN (whisper): ...Sorry, Riyu.

[SFX: clock ticks over. Somewhere a last, single cracker pops far away.]

## SCENE 3 — 2:07 AM — 2:25

[SFX: SILENCE. Then — full ringtone, shockingly loud.]

⚡[fake_call t≈2:30 — full-screen incoming call on listener's device:
**Riya 💜** — with her contact photo, marigold in her hair]
⚡[haptic t≈2:31 — heartbeat_rising, 6s]

NARRATOR: Do baj ke saat minute. Caller ID pe naam aaya. Woh naam... jiska
number Arjun teen din se sirf dekh raha tha. Ring hoti rahi. Uski saans
nahi chali. Phone... utha liya.

[SFX: call connect click. And then — a girl's voice, mid-laugh, alive, the
sound of a terrace at night behind her: distant dhol practice, a dog, wind.]

⚡[volume_duck t≈2:52 to 0.4 — everything ducks under her first word]

RIYA (bright, teasing): Bhaiyaaaa! Do baje tak jaag rahe ho? Kya baat hai,
kal client ne daanta nahi?

[silence]

RIYA: Hello? Arre network... Bhaiya? Sun rahe ho?

ARJUN (barely air): ...Riyu?

RIYA: Nahi, Katrina Kaif. (laughs) Suno na, aaj Maa ke saath chowk gayi
thi — kal Dhanteras hai na — tumhare liye ek cheez li hai. Nahi bataungi,
Diwali pe milega. Hint bhi nahi. Achha ek kaam karo—

ARJUN (breaking): Riya. Riya, tu—

RIYA (stops): ...Bhaiya? Tum... ro rahe ho kya?

ARJUN: Tu kahan hai. Abhi. Exactly.

RIYA (confused, half-laughing): Terrace pe? Apne ghar ke? Neeche Maa kheer
banane ka jhooth bol ke serial dekh rahi hai. Tum batao, tum kahan ho —
studio mein so gaye kya phir se?

NARRATOR: Arjun ne khidki se bahar dekha. Mohalla andhera tha. Diwali ja
chuki thi. Aur phone pe uski behen keh rahi thi ki Diwali aane mein teen
din baaki hain.

ARJUN (slow, careful): Riya... aaj... aaj kya taareekh hai?

RIYA (exasperated sigh): Ho gaya shuru. Bhaiya aaj *Dhanteras se ek din
pehle* hai, Diwali mein teen din hain, aur agar tum is baar bhi kaam ka
bahana banake nahi aaye na, toh main tumhara woh chhota tripod tod dungi.
Bolo aa rahe ho?

[SFX: under Arjun's silence, her terrace ambience continues — she's real,
she's THERE]

ARJUN (whisper, to himself): Teen din...

## SCENE 4 — THE SPIKE — 4:55

[SFX: phone vibration — a different, ugly buzz cutting across the call]

⚡[message_overlay t≈5:00 — news notification slides in over the call UI:
**"Bypass flyover haadsa: scooty sawaar chhaatra ki maut — parivaar ka
aarop, jaanch jaari"** — thumbnail: a crumpled scooty, police tape]

NARRATOR: Usi phone pe. Upar behen ki awaaz. Neeche behen ki maut.

RIYA (light, chattering on): —aur haan, maine abhi terrace se ek reel daali
hai, diye waali, dekhna kitni pyaari — bhaiya? Tum phir chup ho gaye.

ARJUN: Riya, mera ek kaam kar. Mazaak nahi. Apne kamre ki khidki ke paint
pe... nakhoon se... 'A' likh de. Abhi.

RIYA: Haan?? Kyun?

ARJUN: Bas kar de. Please.

RIYA (beat — then, quieter): Tum aaj ajeeb lag rahe ho. Ruko.
[SFX: her footsteps down terrace stairs, a door, a scratch — thin, real:
skreeech, skreech]
Ho gaya. 'A' — Arjun ka A. Ab bataoge kya chal raha hai?

ARJUN: (long breath) Riyu. Jo main bolunga... tu hasegi. Phir daregi. Par
sun na hai. Poora.

[SFX: rising thin static under the line — the first sign of the window]

RIYA (small): ...Bolo.

ARJUN: Diwali ki raat. Gyarah baj ke... (he can't say 58) ...raat ko. Tu
ghar se nikalti hai. Scooty pe. Bypass flyover.

RIYA: Main Diwali ki raat scooty pe kyun jaungi? Diwali pe toh—

ARJUN: NIKALTI HAI. (silence) Aur phir... tu ghar wapas nahi aati.

[SFX: her terrace wind. A long pause. Then—]

RIYA (a laugh that doesn't hold): Bhaiya, yeh... yeh wala mazaak achha
nahi hai. Tumne kabhi aise mazaak nahi kiya.

ARJUN: Isliye keh raha hoon. Kyunki main mazaak nahi kar raha. Main...
main teen din baad se bol raha hoon, Riya. Wahan Diwali ho chuki hai.
Aur wahan— (his voice shreds) —wahan tu nahi hai.

## SCENE 5 — THE WINDOW CLOSES — 7:40

[SFX: static swells in waves now; her voice starts to fray at the edges]

RIYA (scared now, fast): Nahi — ruko — yeh koi prank hai? Dev ne bola
tumse kuch? Bhaiya main pooch rahi hoon—

ARJUN: Teri awaaz kat rahi hai. Riya, sun — jo bhi ho, Diwali ki raat —

RIYA: — bhaiya awaaz — tum — *"proof do"* — (static) — agar tum sach mein
teen din baad se ho na — (static) — toh koi aisi cheez bolo jo abhi tak
hui hi nahi—

⚡[haptic t≈8:20 — single hard knock pattern ×1 as the line SNAPS]

[SFX: dead air. Dial tone. 3:33 AM on a clock somewhere.]

ARJUN: Riya? RIYA—

NARRATOR: Teen baj ke tenti minute. Line aisi kati... jaise kisi ne
kainchi chalayi ho. Arjun wahin khada raha — haath mein phone, phone mein
kuch nahi.

## SCENE 6 — THE PROOF — 8:50

[SFX: bare feet on cold floor, fast. A door that hasn't been opened in three
days — it sticks, then gives.]

NARRATOR: Riya ka kamra. Teen din se band. Agarbatti ki baasi khushboo.
Bistar waise ka waisa. Arjun ne parda hataya. Khidki ka paint.

[SFX: curtain rings. Then — silence held for two full seconds.]

NARRATOR: 'A'.

⚡[screen_dim t≈9:25 — slow dim to 60% as the narration lands]

NARRATOR: Nakhoon se khuda hua 'A'. Kinaaron pe teen din ki dhool jami
thi. Kal raat toh yeh ho nahi sakta tha. Teen din pehle Arjun is kamre mein
tha — tab yeh yahan nahi tha. Yeh nishaan... aaj raat, do ghante pehle,
teen din pehle bana tha.

[SFX: he slides down against the wall, phone still in his hand]

ARJUN (steadying his own breathing, then — resolve): Theek hai. Theek hai
Riyu. Proof chahiye tujhe.

NARRATOR: Uske paas teen raatein thi. Ek ladki thi jo maanti nahi thi ki
woh marne wali hai. Aur ek phone tha... jo is baar silent pe nahi tha.

⚡[haptic t≈10:10 — heartbeat_rising, long, into the checkpoint]
