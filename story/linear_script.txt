RIYA CALLING
A five-episode audio drama. Linear script, default path.

============================================================
EPISODE 1 — MISSED CALL
============================================================

COLD OPEN

[SFX: far-off Diwali crackers, sparse, dying out. A ceiling fan. A phone
vibrates once against wood.]

NARRATOR: Teen missed calls. Gyarah ikatalis. Gyarah saintalis. Gyarah
trepan. Arjun Mishra ne apni zindagi mein hazaar calls uthayi thi — client
ki, bank wale ki, wrong number ki. Jo teen calls usne nahi uthayi... woh
uski behen ki aakhri teen calls thi.

[SFX: a chair creaks. A man exhales — a breath held for three days.]

ARJUN (to himself): Phone silent pe tha. Shaadi ki editing thi.
Deadline thi. ...Deadline.

NARRATOR: Diwali ke teen din baad. Mishra ghar mein ab bhi rishtedaaron ke
jhoothe glass pade the. Maa neend ki goli se soyi thi. Aur Arjun — Arjun
teesri raat se apni behen ka Instagram dekh raha tha.

SCENE — HIS ROOM, 1:50 AM

[SFX: room tone. Phone speaker: a reel plays, tinny — RIYA's voice, bright.]

RIYA (recording): —toh doston, Khoji ke agle episode mein milte hain, tab
tak ke liye — aankhen khuli rakho!

[SFX: the reel loops. And loops.]

ARJUN (quiet): Aankhen khuli rakho. Hm.

NARRATOR: Uski behen ke aakhri shabd, ek reel mein. Unnees saal ki Riya
Mishra. College mein journalism. Closet mein podcast. Scooty pe... Report
mein likha tha — "accident."

[SFX: drawer opens. Glass on wood — a cracked phone placed on the desk.]

NARRATOR: Uska phone. Flyover ke neeche mila tha. Screen pe daraar aisi —
jaise bijli gir ke ruk gayi ho.

[SFX: phone keyboard taps — a message typed to "Riya", never sent:
"kaash maine utha liya hota" — then deleted, letter by letter.]

ARJUN (whisper): ...Sorry, Riyu.

[SFX: clock ticks over. A last, single cracker pops far away.]

SCENE — 2:07 AM

[SFX: silence. Then — full ringtone, shockingly loud.]

NARRATOR: Do baj ke saat minute. Caller ID pe naam aaya. Riya. Woh naam...
jiska number Arjun teen din se sirf dekh raha tha. Ring hoti rahi. Uski
saans nahi chali. Phone... utha liya.

[SFX: call connect click. A girl's voice, mid-laugh, alive; a terrace at
night behind her — distant dhol practice, a dog, wind.]

RIYA (bright, teasing): Bhaiyaaaa! Do baje tak jaag rahe ho? Kya baat hai,
kal client ne daanta nahi?

[silence]

RIYA: Hello? Arre network... Bhaiya? Sun rahe ho?

ARJUN (barely air): ...Riyu?

RIYA: Nahi, Katrina Kaif. (laughs) Suno na, aaj Maa ke saath chowk gayi
thi — kal Dhanteras hai na — tumhare liye ek cheez li hai — nahi bataungi, Diwali pe
milega. Hint bhi nahi. Achha ek kaam karo—

ARJUN (breaking): Riya. Riya, tu—

RIYA (stops): ...Bhaiya? Tum... ro rahe ho kya?

ARJUN: Tu kahan hai. Abhi. Exactly.

RIYA (confused, half-laughing): Terrace pe? Apne ghar ke? Neeche Maa kheer
banane ka jhooth bol ke serial dekh rahi hai. Tum batao, tum kahan ho —
studio mein so gaye kya phir se?

NARRATOR: Arjun ne khidki se bahar dekha. Mohalla andhera tha. Diwali ja
chuki thi. Aur phone pe uski behen keh rahi thi ki Diwali aane mein teen
din baaki hain.

ARJUN (slow, careful): Riya... aaj... aaj kya taareekh hai?

RIYA (exasperated sigh): Ho gaya shuru. Bhaiya aaj Dhanteras se ek din pehle
hai, Diwali mein teen din hain, aur agar tum is baar bhi kaam ka bahana
banake nahi aaye na, toh main tumhara woh chhota tripod tod dungi. Bolo aa
rahe ho?

[SFX: under Arjun's silence, her terrace ambience continues.]

ARJUN (whisper, to himself): Teen din...

SCENE — THE NEWS

[SFX: phone vibration — a different, ugly buzz cutting across the call.]

NARRATOR: Usi phone pe ek news notification chamki — "Bypass flyover
haadsa: scooty sawaar chhaatra ki maut — parivaar ka aarop, jaanch jaari."
Upar behen ki awaaz. Neeche behen ki maut.

RIYA (light, chattering on): —aur haan, maine abhi terrace se ek reel daali
hai, diye waali, dekhna kitni pyaari — bhaiya? Tum phir chup ho gaye.

ARJUN: Riya, mera ek kaam kar. Mazaak nahi. Apne kamre ki khidki ke paint
pe... nakhoon se... 'A' likh de. Abhi.

RIYA: Haan?? Kyun?

ARJUN: Bas kar de. Please.

RIYA (beat — then, quieter): Tum aaj ajeeb lag rahe ho. Ruko.

[SFX: her footsteps down terrace stairs, a door, a scratch — thin, real:
skreeech, skreech.]

RIYA: Ho gaya. 'A' — Arjun ka A. Ab bataoge kya chal raha hai?

ARJUN: (long breath) Riyu. Jo main bolunga... tu hasegi. Phir daregi. Par
sun na hai. Poora.

[SFX: rising thin static under the line.]

RIYA (small): ...Bolo.

ARJUN: Diwali ki raat. Gyarah baj ke... raat ko. Tu ghar se nikalti hai.
Scooty pe. Bypass flyover.

RIYA: Main Diwali ki raat scooty pe kyun jaungi? Diwali pe toh—

ARJUN: NIKALTI HAI. (silence) Aur phir... tu ghar wapas nahi aati.

[SFX: her terrace wind. A long pause.]

RIYA (a laugh that doesn't hold): Bhaiya, yeh... yeh wala mazaak achha
nahi hai. Tumne kabhi aise mazaak nahi kiya.

ARJUN: Isliye keh raha hoon. Kyunki main mazaak nahi kar raha. Main...
main teen din baad se bol raha hoon, Riya. Wahan Diwali ho chuki hai.
Aur wahan— wahan tu nahi hai.

SCENE — THE WINDOW CLOSES

[SFX: static swells in waves; her voice frays at the edges.]

RIYA (scared now, fast): Nahi — ruko — yeh koi prank hai? Dev ne bola
tumse kuch? Bhaiya main pooch rahi hoon—

ARJUN: Teri awaaz kat rahi hai. Riya, sun — jo bhi ho, Diwali ki raat —

RIYA: — bhaiya awaaz — tum — "proof do" — agar tum sach mein teen din baad
se ho na — toh koi aisi cheez bolo jo abhi tak hui hi nahi—

[SFX: the line SNAPS. Dead air. Dial tone. 3:33 AM on a clock somewhere.]

ARJUN: Riya? RIYA—

NARRATOR: Teen baj ke tenti minute. Line aisi kati... jaise kisi ne
kainchi chalayi ho. Arjun wahin khada raha — haath mein phone, phone mein
kuch nahi.

SCENE — THE PROOF

[SFX: bare feet on cold floor, fast. A door that hasn't been opened in
three days — it sticks, then gives.]

NARRATOR: Riya ka kamra. Teen din se band. Agarbatti ki baasi khushboo.
Bistar waise ka waisa. Arjun ne parda hataya. Khidki ka paint.

[SFX: curtain rings. Then — silence held for two full seconds.]

NARRATOR: 'A'. Nakhoon se khuda hua 'A'. Kinaaron pe teen din ki dhool
jami thi. Kal raat toh yeh ho nahi sakta tha. Teen din pehle Arjun is
kamre mein tha — tab yeh yahan nahi tha. Yeh nishaan... aaj raat, do
ghante pehle, teen din pehle bana tha.

[SFX: he slides down against the wall, phone still in his hand.]

ARJUN (steadying his breathing, then — resolve): Theek hai. Theek hai
Riyu. Proof chahiye tujhe.

NARRATOR: Uske paas teen raatein thi. Ek ladki thi jo maanti nahi thi ki
woh marne wali hai. Aur ek phone tha... jo is baar silent pe nahi tha.

============================================================
EPISODE 2 — TEEN DIN, TEEN RAAT
============================================================

COLD OPEN

[SFX: evening mohalla — pressure cooker somewhere, distant dhol practice.
A phone rings. Full ringtone.]

NARRATOR: Dusri raat. Window kal se paanch ghante pehle khul gayi thi —
nau baj ke saat minute pe. Aur aaj... woh proof leke baitha tha.

RIYA (podcast-host voice, mock-formal): Toh, "teen din baad wale bhaiya"
— Khoji special episode mein aapka swagat hai. Aapne kaha tha proof
milega. Recorder on hai. Boliye.

ARJUN (almost smiling despite everything): Agla ek ghanta. Teen cheezein.
Ek — paanch minute mein Sharma uncle taash pe chillayenge, "Rammu ne
patta badla." Do — Maa kheer jala degi, kyunki kheer bahana hai, serial
asli hai. Teen — nau chalis pe... transformer. Poora mohalla andhera.

RIYA: Aur yeh sab tumhe kaise—

ARJUN: Us shaam ki har chhoti baat Maa ne mujhe phone pe sunayi thi.
Baaki news mein aayi thi. Mere liye yeh yaadein hain, Riyu.

[SFX: time-skip montage — Sharma uncle's distant roar "RAMMU!"; a
burnt-milk argument, Maa's guilty "bas do minute ke liye gayi thi";
then 9:40 PM — a dull BOOM. Dogs erupt. The mohalla's hum dies into
darkness. Her breath.]

RIYA (whisper — terrified and lit up): Teen mein se teen. (beat) Bhaiya.
Yeh duniya ki sabse badi story hai. Aur yeh meri story hai.

NARRATOR: Jo kaam bhai ke shabd nahi kar paaye... woh mohalle ke
transformer ne kar diya. Ab woh maanti thi. Aur — bhagwan bachaye — ab
woh iska maza bhi le rahi thi.

SCENE — THE PROOF GAME

[SFX: her terrace, night, blackout-quiet mohalla — crickets and one far
generator. Phone on speaker, her voice close.]

RIYA: Achha. Ek cheez meri taraf se bhi. Tum keh rahe ho tum teen din
aage ho. Toh... jo main abhi karungi, woh tumhare wahan teen din purana
hoga. Correct?

ARJUN: ...Haan. Theek waise, jaise khidki ka 'A'.

RIYA: Toh test karte hain, scientist log jaise. Terrace ki deewar. Chalk
mere haath mein hai. Bolo — kya likhun?

ARJUN: A. B. Do. Zero. Do. Chhe. — AB2026.

RIYA (writing, chalk on brick, saying it letter by letter): A... B...
do hazaar chhabbees. Ho gaya. Ab?

[SFX: present side — Arjun's stairs, taken two at a time. A terrace door.
Night wind. His phone torch clicks on.]

NARRATOR: Arjun apne terrace pe bhaga. Wahi deewar — teen din baad ki.
Torch ki roshni ne deewar ko chhua...

[SFX: held silence]

NARRATOR: AB2026. Chalk ki likhavat. Diwali ke dhuein se maili, ek
baarish khayi hui, kinaron se udti hui. Teen din purani likhavat...
do minute purane haath ki.

ARJUN (hoarse): Riyu. Yeh hai. Yahan hai. Purana ho chuka hai.

RIYA (soft, spooked awe): Maine abhi likha... aur wahan buddha bhi ho
gaya. (beat, then very quiet) Bhaiya. Agar deewar teen din mein buddhi
ho sakti hai... toh meri kahani bhi likhi ja chuki hai na. Wahan.
Tumhare paas.

ARJUN: Likhi hui cheezein... badli ja sakti hain. Deewar tune aaj likhi.
Kahani bhi tu hi likhegi.

[SFX: message sent — a photo of the weathered wall, then typed text to
Zoya: "kal subah 10 baje. sab samjhaunga. isse lab-date karwa lena."]

RIYA: Zoya? Wahi police wali didi? Bhaiya, kal subah kya hai?

ARJUN (beat): Kal subah nahi. Aaj subah. (breath) Riya... aaj din mein
main ek jagah gaya tha. Sun le. Phir plan banayenge.

SCENE — BARAH GHANTE PEHLE

[SFX: transition to daylight — crows, traffic, a chain-link gate dragging
open. Police impound yard: metal ticking in the sun.]

NARRATOR: Barah ghante pehle. Police ka kabaad-khaana, jahan gaadiyan
nahi — adhoori kahaniyan khadi rehti hain. Line se dabi hui cars, bina
number ki bikes... aur ek kinare, tirpal ke neeche, ek scooty. Lavender
rang ki. Jiske handle pe ab bhi ek friendship band bandha tha.

[SFX: tarp pulled back. A camera shutter — once, twice. Arjun's breathing,
too even.]

ARJUN (to himself, working): Front damage... handle mud gaya... theek
hai. Theek hai. (shutter) Piche ka wheel... (the shutter stops) ...Ruk.

[SFX: he crouches; fabric scrapes concrete.]

NARRATOR: Brake cable. Arjun ne hazaar shaadiyon mein hazaar cheezein
zoom ki thi. Ghisa hua cable jab tootta hai, toh resha-resha bikhra hua
hota hai — jhaadu jaisa. Yeh cable... aisa nahi tha.

ARJUN (flat, very quiet): Yeh toh... surgeon ka kaam hai. Ek vaar. Saaf.

NARRATOR: Kati hui brake. Teen missed calls. Aur ek "accident" jo do din
mein file band ho gaya. Arjun ke andar kuch thanda sa ho gaya — dukh se
zyada khatarnaak cheez. Samajh.

[SFX: cut to — a chai stall; glasses clink, a kettle roars, afternoon.]

ZOYA (mid-sip, unimpressed): Toh. Teen din baad se phone aata hai.
(slurp) Khidki pe 'A' likha milta hai. (slurp) Aur ab deewar pe AB2026.

ARJUN: Zoya—

ZOYA: Dekho Arjun — main do cheezon pe yakeen karti hoon. FIR, aur
forensics. Bhoot in dono mein nahi aate. Grief bahut kuch karwata hai,
mere paas cases aate hain aise—

ARJUN (sliding his phone across): Toh forensics hi dekh. Paint teen din
se ukhda hua. Nishaan do ghante purana. Aur yeh — brake cable. Bata,
ghisa hua lagta hai?

[SFX: a long pause. The kettle roars alone. Her glass set down, slow.]

ZOYA (register changed, quiet): ...Yeh cut hai. (beat) Kisne dekha yeh
tumhare alawa?

ARJUN: Koi nahi.

ZOYA: Toh sun. Unofficially bol rahi hoon — yeh photos mujhe bhejo, main
apne bande se cable ki report banwati hoon, bina naam ke. Officially?
(beat) Officially yeh case band hai, Arjun. Aur jis ilaake mein band hua
hai... wahan band cheezein band hi rehti hain. Samajh rahe ho main kya
keh rahi hoon?

ARJUN: MLA.

ZOYA (standing, money on the table): Maine kuch nahi kaha. (beat, softer)
Bhen thi tumhari. Jo bhi hai yeh sab... main dekhungi. Par tum — koi
hero-giri nahi. Jo miley, mujhe do.

SCENE — NAINITAL

[SFX: home, that afternoon — a fridge hum, Maa's slow footsteps, a plate
set down.]

MAA (thin, worn, trying): Khaa le do niwale. (beat) Almari saaf kar rahi
thi subah... Riya ki Nainital wali chunni mili. Jo usne ziddi karke li
thi, yaad hai? Tum dono jhagad pade the wahan, boat pe. (a tired breath
of a laugh) Tumne hi toh...

[SFX: silence. A fork stops against a plate.]

NARRATOR: Nainital. Arjun ne shabd pakda... aur haath khali aaya. Boat?
Chunni? Behen ke saath ka ek poora shehar — tha hi nahi. Jaise kitaab se
kisi ne panna phaad liya ho... itni safai se, ki likha yaad rahe, kahani
nahi.

ARJUN (careful): Haan... Nainital. (beat) Maa, main abhi aata hoon.

[SFX: transition back to night — the live call, her terrace.]

RIYA (mid-thought): —isliye bol rahi hoon, jaise Nainital mein hua tha!
Yaad hai, boat waala scene? Jab tumne mujhse kaha tha ki main—
(she stops) Bhaiya?

ARJUN: ...

RIYA: Nainital. Boat. Hamara Nainital, bhaiya. (silence) Tumhe...
tumhe yaad nahi hai.

ARJUN (barely): Mujhe... naam yaad hai. Baaki...

RIYA (slow, assembling a terrible answer): Khidki ka 'A'. Deewar ka
AB2026. Har baar jab main wahan kuch badalti hoon... yahan tumhare paas
kuch pahunchta hai. Toh phir... keemat bhi toh wahi se kategi na. (beat)
Bhaiya. Jab maine deewar pe likha... tab tumse kya gaya?

ARJUN: Riyu, chhod—

RIYA (not letting go, voice breaking): Nainital gaya. (beat) Toh mujhe
bachate-bachate... tum mujhe kharch kar rahe ho. Thodi-thodi karke.
Main hi jaungi tumhare andar se, bhaiya.

ARJUN (quiet, absolute): Poora shehar de dunga. Har yaad. (beat) Yaadein
dobara ban jaati hain, Riya. Tu nahi banegi.

[SFX: she cries once — short, furious at herself for it — and stops.]

SCENE — KHOJI

ARJUN: Ab tu bata. Brake kaati gayi thi. Yeh "accident" teen din pehle
se likha hua tha. Toh sawaal seedha hai — (beat) — Riya. Kisi se
dushmani?

[SFX: night wind on the terrace. She doesn't answer. Ten full seconds.]

RIYA (finally — flat, old, nothing like her): Sana.

ARJUN: ...Sana? Teri Sana? Riya, uska toh accident—

RIYA: Sana ko gaadi ne nahi maara tha, bhaiya. Gaadi waale ne maara
tha. Fark hota hai. (beat) Rohan Chaddha. MLA ka beta. Us raat woh
peeke tha. Watchman ne dekha tha. CCTV mein tha. Phir watchman mukar
gaya, CCTV ki drive "kharab" ho gayi, Sana ke ghar wale sheher chhod
gaye. Do mahine, bhaiya. Do mahine mein poori ladki mit gayi.

ARJUN (the cold thing again): Aur tu... ek saal se...

RIYA: Ek saal se main uski awaaz ikatthi kar rahi hoon. (beat) Log bol
ke mukar jaate hain. Par awaaz jhooth nahi bolti. Watchman ka voice
note — jab usne gaadi ka rang bataya tha, bikne se pehle. Chaddha ke
garage manager ki recording — headlight ke bill waali. Sana ka aakhri
voicemail. Sab mere paas hai. Ek SD card. Khoji ke recorder mein,
college ke media lab mein — jahan koi nahi dekhta.

ARJUN: Riya... tune yeh sab akele—

RIYA: Crime branch mein ek contact hai, Sana ke mausere bhai ka jaanne
wala. Bas usko pahunchana hai. Aur bhaiya — (a breath) — timing hi sab
kuch hai. Diwali ke agle din subah Rohan ki anticipatory bail ki
hearing hai. Usse pehle yeh file court pahunch gayi na... toh Chaddha
ka beta andar hoga apni hi Diwali pe.

NARRATOR: Diwali ke agle din. Hearing. Arjun ke andar sab kuch ek saath
jud gaya — brake, taareekh, flyover. Uski behen "galat waqt pe galat
jagah" nahi thi. Uski behen sahi jagah pahunchne hi waali thi.

ARJUN (to himself): Isliye Diwali ki raat... Deadline unki hai. Teri
nahi.

RIYA: Kya?

ARJUN: Kuch nahi. (beat) Riya. Yeh baat kisi ko pata hai? Kisi ko bhi?

RIYA: ...Sirf Dev ko. (a small defensive laugh) Woh apna hai, bhaiya.
Usne toh mujhe roka tha, bola tha chhod de yeh sab, dar lagta hai—

[SFX: under her words, a low wrong note enters the score — held.]

ARJUN (even): Haan. Theek hai. (beat that costs him) Sirf... Dev.

SCENE — THE WATCHER

[SFX: she moves — chappal on terrace brick, the railing.]

RIYA (mid-sentence, casual): —waise blackout mein mohalla achha lagta
hai, taare dikhte hain, main toh bolti hoon roz ek transformer—

(she stops. Complete stop.)

RIYA (whisper, close to the mic, very fast): Bhaiya. Neem ke neeche.
Saamne wali gali mein. Koi khada hai.

ARJUN: Ghar ke andar ja. Abhi.

RIYA (whisper): Ruko— uske haath mein kuch— (a tiny flash-click far
away) Bhaiya, woh... woh hamare ghar ki photo le raha hai. Andhere
mein. Blackout mein. (beat) Koi mohalle wala nahi hai. Main jaanti hoon
sabko.

ARJUN (very quiet, very fast): Riya. Neeche. Darwaza. Kundi. Ab.

[SFX: her bare feet FAST down the stairs — a door — a bolt slammed home —
her back against wood, breath held. Distant: a motorcycle kicks awake,
idles once... and fades.]

RIYA (breath returning, shaky): Gaya. (beat) Bhaiya... woh pehle se
jaanta tha main kahan rehti hoon. Woh dekh raha tha.

NARRATOR: Teen din pehle wali raat mein, ek aadmi andhere se photo le
raha tha. Aur teen din baad wali raat mein, Arjun ke paas sirf ek phone
tha, ek behen thi... aur ek raat thi jo abhi khatam nahi hui thi.

============================================================
EPISODE 3 — PARCHHAAI
============================================================

COLD OPEN

[SFX: night insects. A ceiling fan. Under it — faint, constant — the soft
static-breath of an open phone line.]

NARRATOR: Pehli raat, woh line ek ghante chali thi. Do baje se saade teen
baje tak — jaise kisi ne khidki zara si kholi ho. Dusri raat... khidki
khuli reh gayi. Shaam se line lagi thi. Kati nahi. Jaise waqt ko bhi
maloom tha — aaj ki raat lambi hai.

ARJUN (low, into the phone): Battery kitni hai teri?

RIYA (phone-filtered): Assi. Charger jeb mein hai. Tumne bola tha
na — aaj se phone meri jaan hai. Toh jaan ko charge pe rakha hai.

NARRATOR: Teen din mein pehli baar... Arjun hansa nahi. Kyunki aaj raat
plan tha. Aur plan ka matlab tha — woh aadmi. Jo Riya pe nazar rakhe hue
tha.

SCENE — THE BAIT

[SFX: Diwali market — dhol practice, bargaining, a temple loudspeaker,
cycle bells. Riya walking, one earphone in; her breath close on the mic.]

RIYA (murmuring, lips barely moving): Bol bhaiya. Sun rahi hoon.

ARJUN (tight): Seedha chal. Rukna mat, mudna mat. Tu bas... dikhti
reh. Jo bhi hai, usse lagna chahiye tu akeli hai aur tujhe kuch nahi pata.

RIYA: Akeli toh hoon. Aur pata toh sach mein kuch nahi. (beat) Mazaak tha.
Daant mat do.

NARRATOR: Naya bazaar, Diwali se do din pehle — aadhi raat ko bhi bheed
hi bheed. Aur bheed se behtar jaal koi nahi hota. Plan seedha tha: Riya
dikhegi. Peecha karne wala peechhe aayega. Aur sabzi wali gali — jiska
sirf ek hi mooh hai — usse ugal degi.

[SFX: her footsteps turn; market din narrows into a lane — closer walls,
a hand-pump drip, gunny sacks dragged somewhere.]

RIYA (whisper): Gali mein hoon. Ab?

ARJUN: Gin. Dus kadam. Phir paan wale ke peeche ruk ke palat.

RIYA: ...Saat. Aath. Nau. (she stops; breath held; then — a slow exhale)
Bhaiya. Ek bike. Gali ke mooh pe. Wahi bike jo college ke bahar thi —
maine dhyan nahi diya tha tab. Aadmi phone dekhne ka natak kar raha hai.

ARJUN: Photo. Zoom se. Chehra mat — bike. Number plate.

[SFX: camera shutter — once, twice. A sack shifts. A man's cough, close.]

RIYA (whisper, adrenaline turning into that grin you can hear): Mili.
Aadhi plate — MP-zero-four... aage bori hai. Par mili, bhaiya. Ab yeh
sirf mujhe dekhne wala nahi raha. Ab maine bhi isse dekh liya.

NARRATOR: Dar ka rukh badal gaya tha. Shikaar ne shikari ki tasveer
kheench li thi. ...Par shikari ko farak nahi pada. Kyunki usse sirf ek
cheez chahiye thi. Aur woh jaanta tha — woh cheez kahan dhoondhni hai.

[SFX: transition — night deepens; the market fades into a sleeping street.]

NARRATOR: Raat gyarah sattavan. Ghar. Maa apne kamre mein so chuki thi.
Riya jaagti thi — aur line... line ab bhi khuli thi.

SCENE — 11:57 PM

[SFX: a sleeping house. A wall clock. Riya's pen scratching — notes by
lamplight. Her voice low, tired-wired.]

RIYA: ...toh kal subah recorder lab se nikaalti hoon, copies banati hoon,
aur phir tumhare plan ke hisaab se— (yawn) —bhaiya, yeh detective log
sote kab hain?

ARJUN (dry): Retire hone ke baad.

RIYA (small laugh): Retire toh tum abhi ho. Shaadi ki video banate ho.

ARJUN: Haan. Aur teri shaadi ki bhi banaunga. Free mein. Bas tu—

[SFX: CLACK. Every appliance-hum in the house dies at once. The lamp.
The fridge. The fan winds down. True silence.]

RIYA (breath): ...Light gayi.

ARJUN: Poore mohalle ki?

RIYA (moving to the window; whisper): Nahi. Saamne wale ghar ka bulb...
jal raha hai. (beat) Sirf hamari gayi hai, bhaiya.

NARRATOR: Diwali se do din pehle, sheher bhar ke transformer haanfte hain.
